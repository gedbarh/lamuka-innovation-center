WHO WE ARE

Unlocking potential.
Creating possibilities.

Lamuka Innovation Center is a refugee-led initiative based in Nakivale Refugee Settlement, Uganda. Established to create meaningful opportunities, we empower refugees and young people through practical vocational training, entrepreneurship, digital skills and community development.

We believe displacement should never define a person's future. Everyone deserves the opportunity to learn, innovate, build sustainable livelihoods and contribute meaningfully to society.

Through inclusive learning, innovation and community-led initiatives, we transform skills into opportunities and opportunities into lasting change.

Empowering people. Transforming communities.

THE STORY BEHIND LAMUKA

A vision born from community.

Lamuka Innovation Center was born from a belief that every person deserves an opportunity to build a better future, regardless of their background or circumstances.

Established in Nakivale Refugee Settlement, Uganda, Lamuka is a refugee-led initiative working to transform lives through vocational skills, entrepreneurship, digital innovation and community development.

We understand the challenges faced by refugees and underserved communities. That is why our approach places people at the center of development, turning their talents, ideas and ambitions into practical opportunities.

Today, we continue building partnerships, nurturing innovation and creating pathways towards self-reliance and sustainable livelihoods.

We don't just teach skills. We unlock potential and create possibilities.

OUR HISTORY

The Lamuka Journey.

From a vision to a growing movement of opportunity, empowerment and community transformation.

2022
