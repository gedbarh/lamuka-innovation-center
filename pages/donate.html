Every Contribution Creates Opportunity

Lamuka Innovation Center is a refugee-led organization working with refugees and host communities in Nakivale Refugee Settlement, Uganda.

Why Support Lamuka?

Your support can help expand access to practical vocational skills, entrepreneurship, financial literacy, digital education and livelihood opportunities.

Together, we can empower women, young people and vulnerable communities to develop their potential and build more independent and dignified lives.

YOUR SUPPORT MATTERS

What Your Support Can Help Us Do

Skills Development

Support vocational and practical training that equips participants with useful skills.

Entrepreneurship

Help aspiring entrepreneurs develop business ideas and strengthen small enterprises.

Digital Inclusion

Expand access to computer skills, digital literacy and technology-based opportunities.

Community Empowerment

Strengthen opportunities for women, young people and vulnerable community members.
