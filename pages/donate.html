YOUR SUPPORT MATTERS

Together, We Can Create Lasting Opportunities.

Lamuka Innovation Center is a refugee-led organization based in Nakivale Refugee Settlement, Uganda. We work with refugees and host communities to build practical skills, promote entrepreneurship and create pathways towards self-reliance.

Your support can strengthen our work and help more people access opportunities to learn, innovate and build dignified futures.

WHERE YOUR SUPPORT GOES

Help Us Make a Difference.

Your contribution can help strengthen these important areas of our community work.

Skills Development

Support vocational and practical training that equips refugees and young people with skills for employment and self-reliance.

Entrepreneurship

Help aspiring entrepreneurs develop business ideas, strengthen enterprises and create sustainable livelihood opportunities.

Digital Inclusion

Expand access to computer training, digital literacy and technology-based opportunities.

Community Empowerment

Strengthen opportunities for women, young people and vulnerable members of refugee and host communities.

FROM DISPLACEMENT TO OPPORTUNITY

We believe that people are not defined by displacement or difficult circumstances. With access to skills, knowledge and opportunities, individuals can shape their own futures and contribute to stronger communities.
